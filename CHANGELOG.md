# Changelog

All notable changes to **Principle Intelligence and Open Structural Learning** are documented in this file.

This repository develops **Principle Intelligence (PI)** and **Open Structural Learning (OSL)** as a structural-learning framework for extracting reusable Principles from differential evidence, validating them through evidence and counter-evidence, operating over an extensible Open-LHS, externalizing mature structures through PIRP/PIRU, and supporting cumulative Collective Structural Learning.

---

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-17

### Initial Public Research Release

Version **1.0.0** establishes the conceptual foundation, architectural vocabulary, research cases, and visual framework of:

> **Principle Intelligence and Open Structural Learning: From Sparse Differential Evidence to Open-LHS Principles, Two-Way Validation, and Portable Intelligence**

This release is intentionally focused on the **research architecture and structural theory**.

It does not attempt to provide a complete Principle Engine, universal ontology, formal logic system, or large implementation framework.

## Four Core Propositions

### P1 — Small-Sample Structural Leverage

Established the working proposition:

> **As evidence becomes sparse, the value of explicit differences, constraints, counter-evidence, and reusable structural invariants can increase.**

Compact form:

```text
Sample ↓
   ↓
Explicit Structural Leverage ↑
```

This does not claim that neural or statistical learning becomes unusable under small-sample conditions.

Instead, it identifies the increasing value of explicit structural evidence when statistical support is limited.

---

### P2 — Principle Extraction

Defined a **Principle** as:

> A compact and reusable structural invariant, constraint, or relation inferred from differences, transitions, counterfactuals, or critical evidence and intended to remain useful beyond the observations from which it was extracted.

Established the extraction path:

```text
Observation
    ↓
Difference
    ↓
Critical Differential Evidence
    ↓
Candidate Principle
```

---

### P3 — Open-LHS Principle

Introduced the **Open-LHS Principle**:

> The left-hand side of a Principle is not restricted to a permanently predefined symbolic vocabulary.

A Principle LHS may contain:

```text
Observation

Difference / Delta

Metric

Trajectory

CCC

Two-Way CCC Result

Principle

Counter-Evidence

Trigger

Policy

Calling Graph

MDT Structure

World-Model Output

PIRP

PIRU

Runtime-Generated Structure
```

Established the central architectural proposition:

> **LHS vocabulary itself can grow.**

---

### P4 — Principle Lifecycle

Defined a Principle as an:

> **Evidence-bound, context-bound, falsifiable, and evolvable structural hypothesis.**

Established the lifecycle:

```text
Extract
  ↓
Test
  ↓
Support
  ↓
Promote
  ↓
Reuse
  ↓
Challenge
  ↓
Revalidate / Specialize / Split / Merge /
Weaken / Replace / Reject / Archive / Reopen
```

A Principle is therefore not treated as permanent truth.
