# 32. Five Core Propositions

## P1 — Small-Sample Structural Leverage

> Sparse evidence can increase the relative value of explicit differences, constraints, counter-evidence, and structural invariants.

---

## P2 — Principle Extraction

> Reusable structural Principles can be extracted from critical differential evidence.

---

## P3 — Open-LHS Principle

> Principle reasoning need not remain permanently restricted to a predefined symbolic vocabulary.

---

## P4 — Runtime Structural Extension

> Reasoning may generate, retrieve, compose, unfold, or delegate structures required for its own continued reasoning.

---

# 33. Three Design Axioms

## Open-LHS Principle

> The LHS of a Principle may contain any identifiable and structurally compatible intelligence object, including runtime-generated structures.

---

## Runtime Structural Extension

> Principle evaluation may cause missing structural objects to be created or acquired.

---

## Evidence-Bound Structural Growth

# 37. Extended Repository Thesis

Experience produces observations.

Observations expose differences.

Some differences reveal reusable structural relations.

Those relations can become Candidate Principles.

Candidate Principles remain hypotheses until exposed to evidence and counter-evidence.

Validated Principles can become Knowledge Roots.

Knowledge Roots can generate context-bound CCCs.

Runtime execution produces new evidence.

Two-Way CCC can search backward from outcomes toward supporting structure, counter-evidence, alternative causes, and missing conditions.

Open-LHS allows Principles to operate over a structural vocabulary that is not permanently closed.

Runtime Structural Generation allows reasoning to acquire or create missing structures.

UTN can provide identity and typing for unfamiliar structures.

PIRP and PIRU can externalize reusable structural intelligence.

Other agents can discover, inspect, validate, bind, use, and criticize that intelligence.

Their new evidence can Fold Back into shared structural revision.

The resulting system can accumulate not only answers and model parameters, but explicit, portable, criticizable, composable, and evolvable structures.
