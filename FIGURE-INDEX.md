# 9. Why Fig-004 Is a Core Figure

Fig-004 should be treated as one of the visual signatures of the repository.

The figure separates Principle Intelligence from a conventional closed rule system.

A conventional system resembles:

```text
Predefined Vocabulary
        ↓
Predefined Rules
        ↓
Runtime Evaluation
```

Open-LHS Principle Intelligence allows:

```text
Existing Vocabulary
        ↓
Reasoning
        ↓
Structural Gap
        ↓
New Structure
        ↓
Expanded Vocabulary
        ↓
Continued Reasoning
```

The difference is architectural:

> **The reasoning process may alter the set of intelligence structures available to subsequent reasoning.**

This is one of the central claims of Open Structural Learning.
